Is Trading Legal in India? A Checklist to Run Before You Deposit Money

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India 2 min read

The word 'trading' covers very different things. Buying shares through a registered broker is one thing. Depositing money into an app that offers foreign forex with 100x leverage is another. Whether something is legal depends on the product and the platform. Run these six checks before you send any money.

The six-step checklist

StepWhat to checkRed flag
1. Name the productIs it a share, futures and options, a commodity, an INR currency contract, crypto, forex CFD or a binary option?You cannot get a clear answer on what you are buying.
2. Check the brokerSearch the broker's name in the SEBI list of registered intermediaries on sebi.gov.in.Not listed, or reachable only on Telegram or WhatsApp.
3. Check the exchangeThe product should trade on NSE, BSE or MCX and you should receive contract notes.Prices shown only inside the platform's own app.
4. Check forex rulesRetail forex in India is allowed only on INR pairs through exchange-recognised brokers. RBI publishes an Alert List of unauthorised forex platforms.USD or EUR pairs with very high leverage, or CFDs offered to Indians.
5. Check how you payPay only into the broker's own account shown on its official website.Payment to a personal UPI ID or an 'agent'.
6. Check the paper trailYou should get written terms, contract notes and clear withdrawal rules. Profits are taxable.Guaranteed returns or fees to withdraw your own money.

Worked example: the 'guaranteed' app

An app promises 10% profit every week on a Rs 10,000 deposit. If that were true, the money would grow like this: Rs 10,000 × 1.10 repeated for 52 weeks is about Rs 14,20,000 in one year. No real market gives that, which is why 'guaranteed' returns are the first red flag.

Quick reference

ProductStatus in simple words
Shares and F&O through a SEBI-registered brokerRegulated route
INR currency contracts on an exchangeRegulated route
Offshore forex, CFDs and binary optionsNot an authorised route for Indian residents
CryptoNot the same protection as exchange-traded products, and special tax rules apply

Rules change. Always confirm on the official sites of SEBI (sebi.gov.in) and RBI (rbi.org.in), and ask a qualified professional for tax questions.
